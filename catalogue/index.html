How the difficulty levels work

A puzzle's level is the cheapest set of techniques that solves it, not whichever pattern happens to be noticed first. Easy needs scanning and singles; Medium adds locked candidates and pairs; Hard adds triples; Expert adds the fish, single-digit and wing patterns; Extreme forces several different advanced patterns in the same grid. Books marked relaxed pace follow the gentler mass-market scale instead.
