Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 30, 2026

This privacy notice applies to the private Amazon Ads reporting tool operated by Alex Liam. The tool is used only to access, download, and analyze advertising data belonging to the authorized Amazon Ads account.

Information accessed

After authorization through Amazon, the tool may access advertising data made available through the Amazon Ads API, including:

  • Campaign, ad group, keyword, product, and targeting information
  • Advertising impressions, clicks, spend, sales, and attributed purchases
  • Advertiser profile and marketplace information
  • Advertising reports requested by the authorized account owner

How the information is used

The information is used solely for private business reporting, performance analysis, campaign optimization, and record keeping for the Alex Liam Amazon Ads account.

Data storage

Retrieved reports and credentials are stored privately on systems controlled by the account owner. Reasonable measures are used to protect access tokens, refresh tokens, client credentials, and downloaded reports.

Data sharing

Advertising data is not sold, rented, or shared with unrelated third parties. Data may only be processed by service providers when necessary to operate the website or private reporting system, subject to appropriate confidentiality and security obligations.

Data retention

Advertising data is retained only for as long as needed for analysis, accounting, historical comparison, or legal and business record-keeping purposes. Data may be deleted when it is no longer required.

Authorization and access control

Access to Amazon Ads data is granted through Amazon's authorization process. The account owner may revoke the application's access through the relevant Amazon account or advertising account settings.

Third-party services

This tool uses Amazon services, including Login with Amazon and the Amazon Ads API. Amazon's own privacy terms and policies also apply to information processed by Amazon.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the reporting tool, applicable services, or legal requirements change. The current version will always be published on this page.

Contact

For questions about this privacy notice, contact: contact@alexliampuzzles.com

This is a private reporting tool and is not offered as a public service to customers or third-party advertisers.
