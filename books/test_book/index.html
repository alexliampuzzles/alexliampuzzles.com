Stuck? Get a hint

Hints are worked out from the grid you have in front of you — not from the answer key. Start with a nudge and only go further if you need to.

Hint always offers the easiest step available, so you never do more work than you have to. Advanced Hint does the opposite: it picks one of the harder techniques the position will support, for when you would rather learn a pattern than finish the grid — press it again for a different one. Show Candidates pencils in every possible digit for each empty cell so you can see the pattern a hint describes.

Book Puzzle Helper

Pick Hard or Expert, then choose a puzzle number (1–200) matching the book. Either Solve it here or tap Show solution.

Quick steps
  • Choose the section and Puzzle #.
  • Press Solve Puzzle to play it online.
  • Press Show solution for the completed grid.
  • Use Notes for pencil marks.
Keyboard tips
← ↑ ↓ → – move around the grid
1–9 – fill a number
Backspace – erase
N – toggle Notes
C – toggle candidates
H – hint
G – advanced hint
Ctrl/⌘ + Z – undo
Click – select a cell
